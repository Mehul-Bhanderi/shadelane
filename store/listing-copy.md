## Short description (≤80 chars)

## Full description
ShadeLane recommends which window to sit by — left or right — using your route and the sun’s position.

Plan a drive, walk, or bike trip, set departure time, choose shade or sun, and get a clear seat recommendation with a color-coded route.

Features:
• Origin, destination, and optional stop
• Drive / Walk / Bike modes
• Shade or sun preference
• Alternative routes when available
• Timeline scrubber along the ride
• Recent trips saved on your device
• km / mi and light / dark map options
• Shareable advice

Important limitations:
Recommendations use clear-sky solar geometry only. Clouds, buildings, trees, and tunnels are not modeled. ShadeLane is not turn-by-turn navigation or a safety product.

Privacy:
No account required. Location is used only when you tap Use my location. Recent trips stay on your device. Routing and place lookup use third-party map services over HTTPS. See the in-app Privacy Policy.
