Privacy Policy

ShadeLane · Last updated: 11 September 2026

ShadeLane (“the App”) helps you choose a seat side based on sun position along a route. This policy explains what data is used, where it goes, and what we do not collect.

1. Summary

  • No accounts, no advertising SDKs, no analytics or crash reporters by default.
  • Location is used only when you ask (for example, “Use my location”).
  • Trip preferences and recent trips stay on your device.
  • Routing and place lookup use third-party public map services over HTTPS.

2. Data on your device

The App may store locally (WebView / AsyncStorage / localStorage):

  • Recent trip origins, destinations, stops, mode, preference, and departure time
  • Unit preference (km / mi) and map theme (light / dark)

You can clear recent trips from Settings. Uninstalling the App removes local data. Android backup of app data is disabled for ShadeLane store builds.

3. Location

If you tap “Use my location,” the App requests foreground / when-in-use location only. Coordinates are used to set the trip origin on the map. The App does not track you in the background and does not request Always location for its own features.

When reverse-geocoding a map pin or GPS fix, coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim to obtain a place label. You can always type addresses instead of sharing location.

4. Data sent over the network

To provide routes and search, the App contacts these services:

  • Open-Meteo Geocoding — place name search queries you type
  • OSRM public router — coordinates for origin, destination, and optional stop to compute route geometry and travel time
  • Nominatim — reverse geocoding for map pins / GPS origin labels
  • CARTO / OpenStreetMap — map tile images (web / Capacitor builds)
  • Google Maps / Apple Maps — map rendering on some native builds that use the platform map SDK (tile and API traffic may include approximate device region as required by the map provider)
  • Google Fonts / CDN CSS — optional web font and Leaflet stylesheet loads on web-based builds

ShadeLane does not operate its own user database. Third parties process requests under their own terms and privacy policies. Use search and routing reasonably; public demo endpoints may rate-limit heavy traffic.

5. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Your choices

  • Deny location permission and enter places manually
  • Clear recent trips in Settings
  • Stop network use by closing the App or disabling connectivity

7. Contact

Questions about privacy: support@shadelane.app (replace with your real support address before store submission).

8. Changes

We may update this policy when features or third-party services change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
